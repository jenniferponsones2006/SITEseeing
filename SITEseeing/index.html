ABOUT SITESEEING

Making Manila Easier to Explore

SITEseeing is a tourism information system created to help visitors discover destinations, attractions, restaurants, hotels, transportation options, and tour packages around Manila.

There is more to enjoy in the Philippines beyond its famous tourist attractions. You may discover delicious Filipino food, cozy hotels, exciting shopping areas, local products, transportation services, and many other places that can make your journey more enjoyable. From tasting local dishes and exploring busy cities to experiencing peaceful communities and cultural traditions, every part of the Philippines can offer a memorable experience. We created this platform to make it easier for travelers to find and explore the places they may enjoy. Whether you are a local traveler discovering your own country or a visitor experiencing the Philippines for the first time, we hope to help you find new destinations, plan your trip, and enjoy the beauty, culture, and experiences that the Philippines has to offer.
